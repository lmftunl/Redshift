I began by brain dumping a bunch of ideas into google docs, filtering out repeating ideas and consolidating them into a draft that I was comfortable with. Then, I began drafting ideas and fleshing out UI designs in Figjam (https://www.figma.com/board/meiGEtaycmFRgaaCTZTzED/NASA-Space-Apps-Challenge?node-id=0-1&t=JPyErmoblX7eO6xU-1). Finally, at the end of day 1, I was able to obtain a usablee draft for further refinement in Figma design itself.

On Day 2, I continued with creating UI elements, taking inspiration from Kurzgesagt's Star Birds game and Duolingo's use of high-contrast, vibrant and cartoonish elements that I thought would be more engaging with the target audience. By afternoon, I finally finished designing the assets and widgets that would be used. Having no experience in coding, I turned to OpenAI's Codex to build the website for me.
